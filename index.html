Reader Authentication

Sign your requests so the wallet can show the holder who is asking for their data. Each request is signed with your reader private key and carries your reader access certificate.

⚠️ Security Warning:

Your private key is stored unencrypted in this browser's localStorage. Use test keys only: production keys belong in secure hardware (HSM, TPM).
